Eating safeguards

        
Calorie counting can feed restriction and compulsive exercise. These controls limit the harm: numbers can be hidden, goals have a floor, and risky weeks get a gentle check-in.

Words this app avoids

        
"Earned", "burn it off", "cheat meal", "good" and "bad" foods, streak shaming, and celebrating fast weight loss. Going over a target is shown without red, and the ring never turns into a warning.
